Troubleshooting Lab

Guided troubleshooting scenarios.

Select a scenario to review symptoms, likely causes, verification steps, and the fix path.

Scenario 01

DNS outage on VLAN 40

Symptoms: IP pings succeed, but domain lookups fail from the WireGuard-routed VLAN.

  1. Confirm gateway reachability with a direct IP ping.
  2. Test DNS from the affected client and from OPNsense.
  3. Verify Unbound is listening on the VLAN interface.
  4. Apply interface/DNS rule fix and retest.
